Filters can match text: any text, name, sorting name, description, notes, install folder, store game ID, launch path and arguments, and the names of developers, publishers, series, genres, tags and other lists. Compare by contains, starts with, ends with, equals or a regular expression (case-insensitive unless "Aa" is on), or check whether a field is empty or has a value. Every filter and group has a "Not" toggle that turns it into its opposite.
