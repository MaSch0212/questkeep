Settings → General has a Tray group: choose the tray icon (Questkeep logo, default, or a game controller). The tray popup's launcher strip is now Quick launch and holds store launchers and apps you pick, in your order (move, remove, Add to quick launch, Reset to all store launchers); the native tray menu's Quick launch submenu lists the same. Recently played in the tray no longer lists apps.
