Title bar. Add game, Open launcher and Sync sit on the left next to the name; Settings stays on the right by the window buttons. All are icons with tooltips; Sync spins and shows a dot while running.
Command bar (Ctrl+K) in the middle of the title bar finds any game, whatever view is open. Arrows select, Enter opens the game's page, Ctrl+Enter or the button plays or installs it. Empty, it lists recently played games.
Filter this view is a magnifier icon. Click it, press Ctrl+F or just start typing over the games and it opens into a field; while it narrows the list it stays open and tinted, and the count reads “3 of 14 games”. Esc, × or switching views clears and collapses it. It is never saved with a view.
Toolbar icons. Filters (badge = active filters) and one Sort menu for direction and field, like Playnite. Once a view differs from what was saved, an orange dot, Reset and Save appear; Save offers “Update view” and “Save as new view…”.
Questkeep menu. Clicking the name or logo opens one menu with everything: Edit views, Add game, Open launcher, Sync, Settings and Exit.
Right-click the sidebar for Edit views; right-click a view for Rename, Change icon, Duplicate and Delete.
Edit views switches the sidebar to edit mode: drag handles, rename in place, change icons, delete, and New section. Views can be dragged within and between sections, and sections can be reordered.
List and details rows show the game icon instead of the cover and are 36px tall (now 56px).
